# KIST Mixed Reality Lab

**Mixed reality, intelligent virtual agents, and human–computer interaction.**

We are the Mixed Reality Lab (MRLab) in the Center for Artificial Intelligence at the **Korea Institute of Science and Technology (KIST)**. We study how people interact with digital humans and immersive systems, combining AI, real-time interaction, and experiments with users.

[Lab website](https://sites.google.com/view/mrlabkist) · [Research](https://sites.google.com/view/mrlabkist/research) · [Publications](https://sites.google.com/view/mrlabkist/publications) · [Video demos](https://sites.google.com/view/mrlabkist/video-demo) · [Members](https://sites.google.com/view/mrlabkist/members)

## Research

- **Digital humans and conversational behavior:** co-speech gestures, expressive avatars, contextual actions, and multimodal interaction.
- **Mixed and virtual reality:** mobile and wearable interfaces, interactions with physical objects, and remote collaboration.
- **Human perception and evaluation:** social presence, trust, embodiment, and how interface choices affect experience.
- **Immersive navigation:** walking in place and interaction techniques for virtual environments.
- **AI for interactive media:** grounded conversational agents, screenplay-driven animation, and visual style generation.
